le.lore is a private, single-operator tool that produces short anime-lore videos and uploads them to one TikTok account: the operator's own. It has no other users, and it is not offered to the public.

1 Scope

These terms cover the software referred to here as “the tool”, together with its TikTok integration. The tool is operated privately by one person for one TikTok account. There is no sign-up, no account creation, and no service offered to third parties.

Because the tool has a single operator, that person is simultaneously its owner and its only user. References below to “you” mean that operator.

2 What the tool does

  • Selects subjects relating to anime and manga from public sources.
  • Writes a short narration script and renders it as a vertical video with synthetic voice-over and subtitles.
  • Checks the finished video against automated quality rules before anything is uploaded.
  • Uploads the video to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

3 TikTok authorization

The tool uploads only to the account that explicitly authorized it through TikTok's standard OAuth flow. It requests the narrowest set of permissions needed to publish a video and to read back the account's own posting options.

Authorization can be revoked at any time from TikTok's own account settings. Revoking it stops all uploads immediately.

Until the application passes TikTok's review, every upload is created with SELF_ONLY visibility, meaning the video is visible to the account owner alone.
