le. lore

Short videos about the stories behind anime and manga — the details, the origins, the things fans argue about. Published in French on TikTok as @le.lore.

The tool

This site is the official page for the private software that produces and publishes those videos. It is run by one person, for one account. It is not a product, and it is not available to anyone else.

01Picks a subject from public anime and manga sources.
02Writes a short narration and renders a vertical video with voice-over and subtitles.
03Runs automated quality checks, and stops anything that fails them.
04Uploads what passes to the operator's own TikTok account.
